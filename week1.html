What this page is

What it shows. Every [BeLa] member's duel points for the week ending Saturday 3 October 2026: each day Monday to Friday, the Monday–Friday total, Saturday, and the week total. Click any column heading to sort by it, so you can see who is carrying a day and who is quiet on it.

Where the numbers come from. Screen recordings of the in-game Personal Ranking popup: the Daily Rank list for each day, and This Week's Ranking for the totals. Nothing is estimated or modelled. Every figure on this page was read off a game screen.

The one exception. The game shows no Saturday list, so Saturday is worked out as week total minus Monday–Friday. It is arithmetic, not a reading.
